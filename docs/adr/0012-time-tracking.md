# 0012 — Time tracking, timesheets and billable rates

Status: Accepted (owner decisions 255 to 279, 2026-11-26): estimates per team in points or hours, counted by the team's current unit; a time entry is a person's own statement on one work item, by hand or by one server-side timer, stored to the minute; weekly timesheets off by default, approved by a holder of `time.approve` other than the person, frozen by the database once submitted; a project, member, workspace rate cascade with effective dates, one currency, per-entry rounding and rates frozen at approval; money seen only by rate holders; an export in CSV and Excel (275, a departure); never written by an agent, an API key or a job

## Context

Roadmap 3.4 asks to "add time entries (timer and manual), estimates in points or hours per team, timesheets with weekly approval, and a billable-rate cascade of project, member, workspace. Write it fresh; the open-source time trackers are AGPL and are reference reading only." The porting map is `docs/porting/maps/3.4.md`; its open questions 1 to 25 were decided by the owner as decisions 255 to 279 (`docs/HANDOFF.md`; question N is decision 254 + N).

Inflect has no time entries, timers, worklogs, timesheets, estimates or billable rates (`grep -rli` over `src` and `prisma`; its `Task` has no estimate or duration). Three patterns are taken as patterns only: money as integer minor units of an ISO 4217 code held by a CHECK (`AgentSpendingAuthority`), separation of duties on an approval (its policy approval), and, as a lesson not followed, a tenant "currency symbol" that names no currency.

## Decision

1. **Estimates** (decisions 257 to 259). Each team chooses Points, Hours or no estimates (none by default; `Team.estimateUnit`). An item carries two typed columns, `estimatePoints` (a whole number, 0 to 100) and `estimateMinutes` (0 to 60,000, entered as hours and minutes); the team's current unit decides which is shown, written and counted, and the other is kept, hidden and uncounted, so changing the unit, or moving an item to a team with another unit, loses nothing. The estimate is a configurable field of the field sets; templates, project templates' starter items and repeats copy it, and a copy into a team that does not estimate or uses the other unit skips it and says so. The transition record gains both columns, so the cycle burn-up offers Points or Hours beside Items, unestimated items counted and named. Milestone, initiative and goal progress keep counting items (ADR 0002 §17), and the timeline keeps its durations from dates (ADR 0010).
2. **A time entry** (decisions 260, 261, 264). One person, one work item (never a project or nothing), one date, a number of whole minutes (1 to 1,440, never rounded), billable or not, an optional description encrypted under the workspace's key. It may be recorded on open and closed items, never on a deleted item or in an archived project; it stays with the project it was recorded in when its item moves, and survives its item's purge, shown as "Deleted item". It is not a work item, not an activity entry and not a search source (ADR 0008 unchanged).
3. **The timer** (decisions 262, 263). A timer is the person's one time entry with no minutes yet: one per person per workspace, held by the database, so every tab and device shows it; starting another stops and records the first. It counts at most 12 hours and then stops counting, with no job; on Stop the person may lower the time. It counts on the day it started in the person's own time zone, and a week cannot be submitted while one of its timers is on.
4. **Who** (decisions 265 to 267, 278). A `time` permission domain: `view`, `track`, `approve`, `rates`. Owners and Admins hold all four, Members view and track, Viewers view, Guests nothing (decision 204). `time.view` shows who recorded how much on items the caller can see; a person's whole week only to `time.approve`; money only to `time.rates`. A person records only their own time; nobody records time for someone else. Only a signed-in person records, changes, submits or approves time, never an API key, an agent or a job (ADR 0003 §4); an agent may propose an estimate like any field from roadmap 6.2.
5. **Timesheets** (decisions 268 to 270). Off until an Owner or Admin turns on "Timesheets need approval". A week is Monday to Sunday by each entry's date. A person submits their week; anyone holding `time.approve` other than that person approves it or returns it with an explanation; the person may take a submitted week back until it is decided; an approver may reopen an approved week with an explanation. A submitted or approved week's entries are frozen by a database trigger, which admits only the approval's frozen rates, the key rotations' re-wrap and the purge's clearing of the item link. No row means a week not submitted; "Draft" is not a status.
6. **Rates** (decisions 271 to 274). Each project says whether its new entries are billable by default; the person may change it per entry while the week is open. An entry's rate is the project's, else the member's, else the workspace's, each the one in force on the entry's date; none anywhere is "No rate". Every rate has an effective date and a history. One ISO 4217 currency per workspace, chosen before the first rate and not changeable while rates exist. An amount is minutes times the hourly rate in minor units, rounded per entry; approving a week freezes each entry's rate, so later rate changes never alter it. Only holders of `time.rates` see or set rates and amounts, their own included.
7. **Export** (decision 275, the owner's choice C over the recommended A). The entries behind Time totals' filter as CSV or as an Excel workbook, the same rows in both, at most 50,000, amounts only for rate holders, every text cell protected against being read as a formula, audited by format, filter and row count. The workbook is written with `write-excel-file` (MIT) and its one dependency `fflate` (MIT), pinned exactly when 3.4d adds them; no exemption is needed.
8. **Notifications and retention** (decisions 276, 277). A bell to whoever approves when a week is submitted; to the person a bell and an immediate email when it is returned, and a bell when it is approved; a Monday reminder while approval is on; all under the person's preferences, never carrying an explanation's text. Time is kept for the workspace's life; an entry deleted by its person while its week is open is removed, and the audit trail keeps its date and minutes.
9. **Words** (decisions 255, 256): Estimate, Estimate unit, Time entry, Timer, Time spent, Timesheet, Billable, Billable rate, Time tracking settings, Time totals; the verbs Add time, Submit, Approve, Return (ADR 0006, "Time words of sub-phase 3.4").
10. **Delivery** (decision 279): four pull requests, 3.4a estimates, 3.4b time entries and timers, 3.4c timesheets and approval, 3.4d rates, totals and export.

## Consequences

- Points and minutes never mix in a sum; a burn-up, a total or a later capacity view reads one unit, and items of a team in the other unit are named rather than converted.
- A time entry outlives its item and a member's removal: approved weeks never change underneath, and roadmap 8.3's user deletion and retention settings must handle `TimeEntry`, `Timesheet` and `BillableRate`. It outlives its project's purge too: a purged project that time entries name leaves a tombstone, its key, name and deletion kept, read by time alone (owner decision 281, 2026-11-27; `docs/work-items.md`, "The purge and project tombstones").
- Entries record when and for how long a person worked on which item: personal data about work. They are only ever the person's own statements; nothing is inferred from activity, and no screenshot, idle detection or location is collected.
- "Approve" names two decisions, a proposal's and a timesheet's; their objects tell them apart.
- Rates are plaintext integers protected by the permission key, not by encryption, so totals can be summed in the database.
- The export decrypts descriptions for those allowed to read them, so it runs in the mutation tier and is audited; one more runtime dependency, small and MIT, ships with 3.4d.

## What would change this decision

- Customers bill per member per project (a fourth rate level), in several currencies, or in rounded increments.
- A team needs points and hours on the same item, or progress weighted by estimate.
- Imports from other trackers need an API key to write time (roadmap 5.2), or agents are to draft a person's week.
- Retention law or a customer requires purging old entries.
