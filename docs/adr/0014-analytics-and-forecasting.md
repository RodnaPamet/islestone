# 0014 — Analytics and forecasting

Status: Accepted (owner decisions 309 to 334, 2026-11-27): every measure read from the transition record, which gains the state category before each change, and nothing computed stored; an item completed once, at its last move into Done as of the moment of reading; cycle time from the first start, lead time from creation, durations as elapsed working time on the item's own calendar; every item counted, recorded fields judged as recorded and others by current values; day boundaries in the scope's working calendar's zone; time waiting from waiting on and from review steps; a pure, seeded Monte Carlo forecast over the daily completions of the scope's working days, items only, giving 50, 85 and 95 percent dates and the chance by a due date, refusing thin history with its counts; calibration published and shown per scope; personal and shared dashboards with a default in code; an `analytics` permission domain with per-person time waiting behind `analytics.people` and no per-assignee productivity measures; agents read-only; live reads with stated bounds

## Context

Roadmap 3.6 asks to "add analytics: cycle time, lead time, throughput, cumulative flow, burn-up. Add a forecasting module, pure and seeded, that runs Monte Carlo simulation over historical throughput to give a completion-date distribution for any scope, shown as dates at 50, 85 and 95 percent. Refuse to forecast with fewer than a stated minimum of completed items and say so. Add a configurable dashboard on the ported dashboard grid." Owner decision 76 adds time waiting per party, read from waiting on's recorded changes (decision 107). Hardening H3 asks for a published back-test and for determinism and honesty on thin history. The porting map is `docs/porting/maps/3.6.md`; its open questions 1 to 26 were decided by the owner as decisions 309 to 334 (`docs/HANDOFF.md`; question N is decision 308 + N), every one as recommended.

- **Verified** (Islestone at `0937df5`). `WorkItemTransition` holds the item's picture after every insert and every change of state, cycle, milestone, project, deletion, waiting on and estimates (migration `20261220120000_estimates`), written only by a SECURITY DEFINER trigger, kept after the purge (decision 66). It records no assignee, type, label, parent, goal or date. Waiting on is cleared while a review round is open (decisions 152 and 153); the review rows are never rewritten once ended (ADR 0009 §4). The scale seed writes one transition per item, at seed time. The plan-check coverage guard does not cover the record.
- **Verified** (inflect-compliance at `5528464fc54a8681956f3b9ce23ca9c075f0420e`, BUSL-1.1, copying allowed by ADR 0001 D1). `DashboardGrid` and `DashboardWidget` are already mirrored byte for byte; the org dashboard's widget schema (bounded positions and a discriminated union), its view and edit page, its mulberry32 generator (`src/lib/fair-math.ts`), its loss-exceedance curve and its histogram are copied and adapted; its default preset, picker, dispatcher and calibration back-test are taken as patterns. Its Monte Carlo's defects (a clock read in the pure function, a silent default seed, zeros for an empty population) are not repeated.
- **Verified** (jira-auto-scheduler at `39323ed28d7d7e2223853f67a098dd9a23f2773f`, MIT, through `docs/porting/oss/jira-auto-scheduler.md`). It forecasts from estimates, not throughput. Its Monte Carlo shell (trials, sorted outcomes, percentiles, a histogram and the chance of finishing after a date, a refusal when there is nothing to forecast) is taken as a pattern only; no code copied.
- ADR 0006 bans "Velocity", "Burn-down", "Confidence", "Report" and "Card", and banned "Dashboard" under My Work. Plane and the other inspiration-only projects (roadmap §2.2) were not read.

## Decision

1. **The record is the source** (decisions 310, 331). Every measure reads `WorkItemTransition` (and, for review waits, the review rows), never a stored series; nothing computed is stored, cached or audited. The record gains `fromStateCategory`, the state category before the change (NULL on the insert row), written by the trigger and backfilled once, with a partial index over completion rows. Reads are live SQL over the record, bounded (20,000 items for flow and time waiting, 50,000 completions; a 422 with the count above, never cut), plan-checked and budgeted in the nightly run; pre-aggregation is a fallback only if they fail.
2. **Completion** (decision 311). An item is completed as of a moment when its picture then is in a Done state and not deleted; it counts once, on the day of its last move into Done at or before that moment. A reopen moves the count to its later completion, a deletion retracts it, a cancelled item never counts. A back-test reads only rows at or before its origin.
3. **Durations** (decisions 312, 313). Cycle time runs from an item's first move into Started to its completion; lead time from its creation (its first recorded row); an item done without being Started has no cycle time and is counted as "Done without starting". Age is an open, started item's time since its first start. Each is elapsed working time on the item's own working calendar (its team's, else the workspace's): the time between two moments less every whole non-working day, in days to one decimal, through the scheduling module's calendar functions (ADR 0010 §1). Percentiles are nearest rank everywhere.
4. **What counts** (decisions 314, 315). Every item counts once, parents and sub-items alike, as progress does (decision 212). History judges recorded fields (project, team, cycle, milestone, state, deletion) as recorded at each moment, so a moved item counts in each project for its time there; every other filter judges the item's current values. Archived projects' history counts; deleted projects' does not; projects the caller cannot see never count.
5. **Days and zones** (decision 316). Day boundaries use the scope's working calendar's zone (one team's calendar for a one-team scope, else the workspace's); durations use each item's own calendar. The cycle burn-up follows the same rule (its team's calendar zone, which defaults to `NOTIFICATIONS_TZ`). Nightly jobs stay on `NOTIFICATIONS_TZ` (decision 191). Dates are epoch days.
6. **Time waiting** (decision 317). A wait is a run of an item's rows naming the same person in waiting on while it is live, or a reviewer's time from their step's opening to their latest response, their withdrawal or the step's end. Each wait counts its overlap with a window as elapsed working time. Per person: the total waited, the items, the median and 85th percentile of ended waits, and what waits on them now; removed members as one anonymous row.
7. **The forecasting module** (decisions 318 to 323). Pure and client-safe. For a scope's open items (live, open, in visible, unarchived projects), counted in items only, it samples the daily completions of each working day of the scope's calendar over the last 12 weeks by default (4, 8, 12 or 26; never before the history's first record; a non-working day's completions fold into the next working day), from the completions of the scope's projects (a cycle: its team's), changeable to a subset, the team or the workspace. It assumes all of that throughput unless the person chooses 75, 50 or 25 %, and no new items, and prints both. It runs 10,000 trials, working day by working day, on mulberry32 seeded from a hash of its inputs, and gives the nearest-rank 50, 85 and 95 percent dates through `addWorkingDays`, the chance of finishing by the scope's due date, a histogram and a "Chance finished by" curve, with what it was based on and the seed. It refuses, with no date and a sentence naming the counts, below 20 completed items in the window or 20 working days of recorded history, with nothing remaining, and when even the 50 % date is beyond 520 working days; below 50 items it adds a caution. It never writes, stores or invents a date (principle 4).
8. **Where forecasts appear** (decision 324). On milestone, project, cycle, goal and initiative pages, as a "Forecast" action for the Work items list's current filters, and as a dashboard widget.
9. **Calibration** (decision 325). The back-test forecasts the date of the next 10, 25 and 50 completions from every origin five working days apart, with the history as it stood at the origin, and scores the hit rate of each percentile with its count and a binomial band. It is published in `docs/analytics.md` from the scale seed's history; a test holds the 85 % hit rate between 80 and 90 % over at least 200 scorable origins; each forecast shows its own scope's record once it has 10 scorable origins.
10. **Dashboards** (decisions 326 to 328). Personal dashboards for anyone holding `analytics.view`, Viewers included; shared ones created by holders of `analytics.share`, changed only by their creator, deletable by an Owner or Admin (audited `DASHBOARD_MODERATED`); a default composition in code until someone customises. Widgets: Counts, Throughput, Cycle time (with age), Lead time, Cumulative flow, Burn-up, Forecast, Time waiting, each with its table. Each widget holds its own copy of the list's filters and a relative window; every read runs under the viewer's visibility and keys. A layout is replaced as a whole under a version, and every dashboard write is audited.
11. **Who** (decisions 329, 330). An `analytics` permission domain: `view` (Owners, Admins, Members, Viewers), `share` (Owners, Admins, Members), `people` (Owners, Admins); Guests nothing (decision 204). Everyone sees their own time-waiting row; others' rows need `analytics.people`. No throughput, cycle time or count is grouped by assignee; an "assignee is" filter still narrows any chart.
12. **Agents** (decision 332). An agent reads analytics and forecasts only through 6.4's read tools, within its authority (ADR 0003 §4); never per-person time waiting; never creates or changes a dashboard. A model explains a forecast from its result and inputs and states no date of its own.
13. **Words** (decision 309): Analytics, Throughput, Cycle time, Lead time, Age, Cumulative flow, Time waiting, Forecast, the "N% likely" dates, Dashboard, Widget (ADR 0006, "Analytics words of sub-phase 3.6").
14. **Sources and delivery** (decisions 333, 334). jira-auto-scheduler as a pattern only; inflect-compliance's pieces named above copied and adapted with `PROVENANCE.md` rows; no `NOTICE` change. Four pull requests: 3.6a the record's column, the measures and the Analytics page; 3.6b time waiting; 3.6c forecasting and calibration; 3.6d dashboards.

## Consequences

- A past week's throughput can change when an item done that week is reopened or deleted: history is shown as it stands. The back-test does not see that drift, because it reads only what existed at each origin.
- Filters on fields the record does not hold (labels, type, assignee) apply today's values to the past; `docs/analytics.md` says so.
- A forecast is only as good as the team's recent stability, and assumes the scope keeps its share and gains no items; the printed assumptions and the per-scope record say so.
- Time waiting per person measures responsiveness; it is visible beyond one's own row only to `analytics.people` holders, and no per-assignee productivity figure exists.
- Shared dashboards can show different numbers to different viewers, each seeing only what they may.
- The record grows by one column; the trigger stays its only writer, and a later column joins it as before.

## What would change this decision

- An estimate-driven forecast over the scheduling engine is wanted (roadmap 7.5, jira-auto-scheduler's model).
- Live reads miss their budgets at 100,000 items (pre-aggregation, with fewer filters on flow).
- 8.4 tags guests with their firm (waits grouped per firm), or 6.3 makes an agent session a waiting-on party.
- A need to judge assignee, type or labels as recorded (new record columns).
- Customers ask for per-assignee figures, or a jurisdiction restricts time waiting per person.
