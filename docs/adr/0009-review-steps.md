# 0009 — Review steps

Status: Accepted (owner decisions 148 to 165, 2026-11-20): review steps, rounds and reviewers on a work item, Signed off and Returned, an indicator with a per-type Done check, explicit sending, lists resolved when a step opens, any active member who sees the project as a reviewer but never the item's assignee (158), a response changeable until its step completes (162), and only people review

## Context

Roadmap 2.8 asks for ordered review steps on a work item: each step names reviewers (members, or a recipient list from 2.7), says whether all or any must respond, and is due a number of working days after it opens; while a step is open the item waits on its reviewers who have not responded; a returned step ends the round and a new round of the same steps follows, the earlier one read-only; default steps per type are copied at create; only people respond (owner decision 80). The porting map is `docs/porting/maps/2.8.md`; its open questions 1 to 18 were decided by the owner as decisions 148 to 165 (`docs/HANDOFF.md`).

- **Verified** (2026-11-20, Islestone at `456f26b`). ADR 0002 §1 said "per-type workflows, parent rules or review steps would each be a new decision", §3 "No reviewer gate in this ADR", and §14 "roadmap 2.8's review steps will set it [waiting on] too". `WorkItem.waitingOnUserId` names at most one member, with a CHECK that a closed item names nobody (migration `20261128120100_waiting_on`).
- **Verified** (inflect-compliance at `5528464fc54a8681956f3b9ce23ca9c075f0420e`). inflect has no multi-step review. Four of its patterns are re-used, none of its code: the claim on a still-pending row (`PolicyApprovalRepository.decide`), the stale-decision refusal (`decidePolicyApproval`), the one-way stamp trigger (`access_review_connected_decision_one_way`, migration `20261018130100_agent_recertification_campaigns`) and "zero is never complete" (its `CLAUDE.md`, legacy access invariant 5). Recorded in `PROVENANCE.md` as pattern only.
- A review step is the human counterpart of the proposal queue (roadmap 6.2), not part of it: a proposal reviews an agent's change, a review step reviews a person's work (decision 80).

## Decision

1. **The model.** A work item may carry review rounds (`WorkItemReviewRound`); a round holds ordered review steps (`WorkItemReviewStep`); a step names reviewers, members and recipient lists (`WorkItemReviewStepList`), and says whether all or any one of them must sign off; it is due a number of working days after it opens. Steps run in order; reviewers within a step respond in parallel. Each person asked is one row (`WorkItemReviewer`) carrying their response. At most one round of an item is planned or open at a time.
2. **Outcomes** (decision 148). A reviewer responds **Signed off** or **Returned**, with text: required to Return, optional to sign off (decision 157). The text is its own encrypted column on the reviewer row, never a comment: it is not searched (ADR 0008 unchanged), carries no mentions, and an admin cannot remove it.
3. **A response may change until its step completes** (decision 162). A reviewer may change their response, outcome or text, while the step is open. The step completes when every reviewer still asked has signed off and at least one has (an "all" step), at the first sign-off (an "any" step), at the first Return (decision 156: one Return returns an "all" step at once and withdraws the others still pending; in an "any" step the first response decides it either way), by a skip, or by the round's withdrawal. From then on its responses and the step itself are frozen. Each change is audited with ids, never the text, and is decided under a lock on the round, so it cannot race the step's completion. Changing a response to Returned returns the step at once; changing a sign-off may complete it.
4. **A Return ends the round; nothing is rewound.** A returned round's steps, reviewers and responses stay exactly as they were, read-only, held by database triggers; a new round with the same steps (names, modes, working days, named members and lists, never the previous round's resolved people) is planned at once and waits until a person sends it (decision 155). **A round that has ended is never rewritten**: no update or delete of its rows is accepted, apart from the key rotations' re-wrap of the encrypted response text and the item's purge.
5. **Rounds start explicitly** (decision 155): "Send for review" opens a planned round's first step. A step opens only with at least one reviewer who may respond: **zero is never complete**. Sending refuses a round whose steps resolve to nobody (`NO_REVIEWERS`), and a later step that opens, or an open step that is left, with nobody stays open and flagged, never completed; a person skips it, adds a reviewer or withdraws the round.
6. **Recipient lists resolve when their step opens** (decision 154), through the one resolver `resolveListRecipients`: the people then become reviewer rows ("via" the list); a later change to the list asks nobody new.
7. **Who may be a reviewer** (decision 158, the owner's choice B). Any active member who sees the item's project and holds `work_items.view`, Viewers included, **but never the item's assignee**: a person does not review their own work, so a one-person team cannot review its own work. The rule is held three times: naming the assignee as a reviewer is refused when steps are edited (`REVIEWER_IS_ASSIGNEE`), a list's resolution at open drops the assignee, and the respond usecase refuses the assignee (a 403, audited). When the item's assignee changes to a person who is a pending reviewer, their reviewer row is withdrawn (`BECAME_ASSIGNEE`), audited, and the step re-evaluated.
8. **Waiting on** (decisions 152 and 153). ADR 0002 §14's column stays one person. While a round is open the item waits on the open step's pending reviewers, read from their rows, not from the column: the column is cleared when a round opens and names the assignee (an active member, the item open) when the round completes or is returned; a withdrawn round sets nothing. A person does not set waiting on by hand while a round is open (409 `REVIEW_ROUND_OPEN`), and 6.3's agent-session reference follows the same rule.
9. **An open round never makes a reviewer accountable for the item**: the assignee stays the one accountable person (ADR 0003 §1).
10. **Only people review.** A reviewer is a person with an active membership, responding as themselves. An agent, an automation rule, an API key, an import, the system user, and a person acting for another (an admin included) never respond to a step and never count towards one. A delegate may at most draft a review comment as a proposal (roadmap 6.2) that the named reviewer approves as their own comment; approving it records no response. This stands beside ADR 0003 §4's "agents propose, people approve" and is not changed by any autonomy rung.
11. **Who changes, skips and closes steps** (decision 150). Anyone with `work_items.edit` edits steps not yet opened, sends a round and withdraws it. Skipping a step, closing one whose reviewers left (a skip with reason `NO_REVIEWERS_LEFT`) and removing a pending reviewer from an open step take the item's assignee, whoever sent the round, or an admin (`admin.manage`), and are audited. No new permission domain (decision 160): responding is under `work_items.view` plus being the named reviewer.
12. **The item stays editable during a round** (decision 163). Each response records the item's `version`, so the record says what was reviewed and the review section can show "changed since signed off".
13. **Due dates in working days** (decision 151): Monday to Friday, no holidays, computed in `NOTIFICATIONS_TZ` when the step opens and stored date-only; roadmap 3.1's calendars replace the constant for steps opened after it.
14. **Gate or indicator** (decision 149). Review is an indicator by default. A work item type may require its review to finish before an item moves to Done (built in 2.8c); cancelling is never held. Closing or deleting an item withdraws its open round; a reopen or a restore does not reopen it.
15. **Default steps** (decision 159) belong to a work item type across the workspace, set under `admin.manage`, naming members and workspace lists, and are copied onto an item by every create path, templates, intake and repeats included; none opens them, and changing them changes no item (built in 2.8c).
16. **Notifications** (decision 161): review requested, review due, returned and completed; immediate email is offered for requested and returned, the reminders come from the 08:00 due job. None carries the response text.

## Consequences

- Every state path ends an open round on close, and every item delete withdraws one; member removal withdraws the member's pending reviewer rows and re-evaluates their steps; the purge removes rounds with the item.
- Waiting on has two sources while a round is open (the column, empty, and the reviewer rows); the "Waiting on me" section and the waiting-on lane keep reading the column, and My Work gains "To review" (roadmap 2.8b) over a `reviewerId` filter.
- A review step is not a proposal and does not use the proposal queue (6.2).
- Conditional steps arrive with roadmap 5.1's conditions ("an automation never responds to a review step"). Working days are Monday to Friday until 3.1.
- A reviewer who loses sight of the project after a move stays asked until 8.4's guest grants revisit it; their response is refused while they cannot read the item.

## What would change this decision

- Teams need reviewers who are not members (8.4's guests), a reviewer acting for another, or sign-offs that certify an exact version (decision 163's option B: lock title and description during a round).
- Review gates are wanted beyond the per-type Done check, or conditional steps (5.1) need a step to open on something other than the previous step's completion.
- A calendar other than Monday to Friday is needed before 3.1.
