## 0. Findings that shape the plan

1. **There are 109 GENERIC files: 101 under `src/components/ui` and 8 under `src/lib/hooks`.** Ten of them are 13-line re-export shims onto `@inflect/ui` (§7). The roadmap's "494 GENERIC" also counts the 376 files already in `packages/ui` and the layout and app-shell roots, which belong to 1.7.
2. **The GENERIC set is not import-closed, so the roadmap rule "leave MIXED out" cannot hold literally.** 16 GENERIC files import a MIXED, COUPLED or non-root file directly. Three steps close the rest:
   - Part A, plus two neutral zero-import leaves (`src/lib/ui-storage.ts`, `src/lib/format-bytes.ts`), plus two Islestone-owned stubs (`charts/index.ts`, `icons/index.tsx`): **97 of 109** compile.
   - Also copying **14 MIXED files**, `src/lib/filters/filter-presets.ts` and the zero-import `src/lib/text-utils.ts`: **106 of 109** compile. All 14 are coupled by prose (comments and JSDoc) or by English UI strings only. None imports a compliance domain module. The one mechanical domain-import, `use-filter-presets.ts` → `@/lib/filters`, is UI-preference persistence, and the audit cleared it.
   - The last 3 are `dashboard-widgets/{index,types,TargetLine}`. They hang off the org-dashboard widget system (`ChartRenderer`, the COUPLED `WidgetPicker`) and the KPI-card family. Defer two and cut the barrel. `DashboardGrid` and `DashboardWidget` are self-contained and are copied.
3. **Keep the inflect paths.** The copies import `@/components/ui/...`, `@/lib/hooks/...`, `@/lib/ui-storage` and `@inflect/ui/...`. A byte-identical copy therefore needs:
   - the same paths under Islestone `src/`;
   - `tsconfig` `paths` `"@/*": ["./src/*"]`;
   - the workspace package still named `@inflect/ui` (part A already keeps that name).
   Moving these files into `packages/ui` would mean editing every specifier and would break the hash sync.
4. **Hash-sync these files the way part A syncs the package.** They need a second manifest, the playerz model (`docs/shared-ui-policy.md`, "For a consumer"). It covers two kinds of path, and a root-scoped "unlisted file" check like `ui-drift verify` sees only the first:

Inside them it must name the **Islestone-owned** files, so that `verify` does not report them as unlisted: the two stubs (`charts/index.ts`, `icons/index.tsx`) and any Islestone component added later. It must also name the two upstream files deliberately not taken (`filter/GUIDE.md`, `table/GUIDE.md`, §1), so that a compare against upstream does not report them as missing.
   - **Individual files outside the roots**, listed one by one, because no root check reaches them: `src/lib/format-bytes.ts`, `src/lib/text-utils.ts` and `src/lib/filters/filter-presets.ts` as hash-tracked byte-identical copies, and `src/lib/ui-storage.ts` as **Islestone-owned**, with its fork recorded in `PROVENANCE.md`.
   The `ui-storage.ts` fork is the `UI_STORAGE_PREFIX` constant on line 42, which `docs/shared-ui-policy.md` names "the one constant a downstream product changes", plus the docblock lines that spell the prefix out (19, 28-31 and 47-65; line 12 is upstream history). ## Appendix: method

- `graph.js` resolves every import from the 626 classified files, transitively: `import`, `export … from`, `import()`, `require`, `jest.mock` and `import("…")` types, through `@/`, `@inflect/ui/` and relative paths. - `tokens.js` holds the theme-key scan, `i18n.py` the catalogue scan, and `tests.js` and `testclass.py` the test classification. `mech.js` re-derives the classification map's mechanical couplings.
