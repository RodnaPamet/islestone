## Summary

- **What moves.**
  - **Jobs.** The BullMQ jobs core: one typed queue, an executor registry with two registrations, repeatable schedules with orphan reconciliation, idempotent fan-out, page draining, the tenant-scoped job lookup and the Redis heartbeat. With it go the three process entry points (`scripts/worker.ts`, `scripts/scheduler.ts`, `scripts/worker-healthcheck.ts`), the esbuild bundler (`scripts/build-worker.mjs`), and the job-context builders (`context-system.ts`, `tenant-system-user.ts`).
  - **Observability.** The rest of the barrel beyond what 1.3 pulled forward: tracing, metrics, OTel bootstrap, Sentry, `runJob`, job-trace propagation, the process logger, graceful shutdown with its budget, business metrics, and web vitals with their beacon route.
  - **Notifications.** The outbox (enqueue, claim-before-send drain, settings) and the owner-grouped digest dispatcher. Also the in-app bell write path (assignment, watcher, inserted ids, the in-process bus, SSE) and the notification REST, SSE and settings routes.
- **What is cut.**
  - 69 of the 80 files in `src/app-layer/jobs`: 20,066 lines of compliance, identity, agent, integration and evidence jobs (full list below). Ten files are ported and `scheduler.ts` is skipped as unused infrastructure.
  - 76 of the 78 keys in `JobPayloadMap` and `JOB_DEFAULTS` (`types.ts`), and 76 of the 78 `register()` calls in `executor-registry.ts`. Only `health-check` and `notification-outbox-flush` stay.
  - The leaver, agentic and SCIM notification emitters.
  - Every compliance email template. The prompt says to replace the templates with react-email, so templates are rewritten, not copied.
  - The compliance metric families in `metrics.ts` and `business-metrics.ts`.
- **Deviations from the roadmap prompt, stated on purpose.**
  - **The registry is not empty.** The prompt asks for "an empty executor registry" plus "one sample job". The map keeps two registrations: `notification-outbox-flush` is the sample job, and `health-check` (every 2 minutes) is what keeps the worker heartbeat alive. The worker writes the heartbeat (`worker-heartbeat.ts` `beat()`) from BullMQ's `completed` event, never from a timer (`scripts/worker.ts` L346–354: a timer keeps firing through a severed Redis connection and reports a wedged worker as healthy, #2745), so it needs a job that completes more often than the 8-minute TTL. Without `health-check` the only job is the 10-minute outbox flush, and the 1.8 compose healthcheck would report a live worker as dead between ticks. Both are infrastructure, neither is compliance. If the owner wants the literal reading, the heartbeat TTL must rise above four outbox ticks (over 40 minutes), and `worker-heartbeat-wiring` changes with it; do not move `beat()` to a timer.
  - **The scope's "preferences models" are reduced.** `UserNotificationPreference` and the `NotificationDelivery` enum are deferred to 2.7. `git grep userNotificationPreference -- src` finds no reader at the commit, and 2.7's "per-event preferences and digests" needs a different shape. The tenant-level preference that has a reader (`TenantNotificationSettings.mutedInAppTypes`) is ported.
- **Size.** The manifest has 81 source files:
  - 13 COPY (647 lines);
  - 48 COPY+EDIT (12,358 source lines, about 8,100 after the cuts; this includes one ESLint rule);
  - 3 REWRITE (3,078 source lines, about 650 lines of react-email and TypeScript after);
  - 17 SKIP (6,174 lines; two of them, the NIS2 incident-notification route and the bell, are listed only so the notification surface is complete);
  - plus three Prisma fragments and two edits to files other sub-phases own (`auth.prisma`, `src/middleware.ts`).
  - About 330 lines are new: the Prisma file, a migration, `non-retryable.ts`, `in-app-types.ts`, the email components, the in-app mute check in the two emitters, and a sample-job test.
  - **About 9,700 lines of app code land.** Tests: 87 source files (15 COPY, 68 COPY+EDIT, 4 REWRITE; 17,311 source lines), plus three new.
- **Prisma.** Three tenant tables: `Notification`, `NotificationOutbox` and `TenantNotificationSettings`, with three enums. They come in one fresh migration with RLS and the system-user CHECKs.
- **New dependencies.** Runtime: `bullmq`, `ioredis`, `pino`, `nodemailer`, `@sentry/nextjs`, nine `@opentelemetry/*` packages, `dotenv` and `@react-email/components`. Dev: `esbuild`, `tsx`, `pino-pretty`, `@types/nodemailer`, `ioredis-mock` and `web-vitals` (types only).
- **Gate.** `docs/adr/0001-code-provenance-and-licences.md` "Interim rule" item 6 names 1.6 among the steps that wait for decision D1. Islestone's `CLAUDE.md` now records the owner decisions of 2026-11-18: copying from inflect-compliance is allowed, with every copied file recorded in `PROVENANCE.md`, and ADR 0001 is to record the decisions (see `docs/HANDOFF.md` until it does). Each ported file still gets its `PROVENANCE.md` row in the same pull request.

## Tests to bring

Islestone conventions apply to every ported test (Islestone `CLAUDE.md`):
- guards take their population from `gitFiles()` (`tests/helpers/git-files.ts`), never from `readdirSync` / `repoFiles()`;
- each guard proves itself against a planted fixture;
- `@/env` maps to `tests/mocks/env.ts`, so add `REDIS_URL`, `OTEL_*` and `SMTP_*` defaults there only if a module reads them at load.
